About OSA-IDE Nigeria Limited

Company profile

A locally owned Nigerian company.

OSA-IDE Nigeria Limited is incorporated and registered in Nigeria, specialising in building and civil engineering construction, general project management and supervision.

Established in 2016 and formally registered in 2020, the company has built a track record through its commitment to quality, professionalism and successful project delivery.

Our directors and site management teams are dynamic professionals with extensive experience and specialised skills across engineering, building construction, environmental services and project management.

We take pride in delivering projects on schedule, within budget and to the highest standards of quality and workmanship. Our clients include government institutions, corporate organisations, property developers and private individuals.
